Privacy Policy

Trim for iPhone. Last updated 19 August 2026.

Operator: Marvin Beckmann · marvinbeckm@gmail.com

What Trim stores

Routines, workouts, history, and settings are saved only on your iPhone. Trim does not create an account and does not upload workout data to a server.

Deleting the app deletes that local data. Cancel any subscription separately in iOS Settings.

Payments

If you buy Trim Pro, Apple processes the payment. Trim uses RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat sees purchase tokens and App Store identifiers, not your workout log.

Apple’s privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

What we do not use

  • No advertising or tracking SDKs
  • No analytics SDK
  • No cloud login
  • No HealthKit
  • No third-party exercise API in the store build

Legal basis (GDPR)

Local workout data is processed on your device to provide the app (performance of a contract). Subscription status is processed so we can unlock Pro (performance of a contract) via Apple and RevenueCat.

Your rights

You can access or delete local data by using the app or deleting it. For payment records, use your Apple ID. Email marvinbeckm@gmail.com for privacy questions.
