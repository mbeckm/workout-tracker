Privacy Policy

Trim for iPhone. Last updated 10 October 2026.

Operator: Marvin Beckmann · marvinbeckm@gmail.com

What stays on your iPhone

Your plans, workouts, history, body check-ins, custom exercises and settings are saved only on your iPhone. Trim has no account and no sign-in, and it does not upload your workout data to any server, including ours. The one exception is importing a plan, and only when you ask for it: see below.

The exercise list is built into Trim. The app does not contact exercise databases or image servers, and it does not download exercise images or videos.

Like any app's data, this may be included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or computer), which Apple controls under your Apple Account settings. Trim does not read those backups.

Importing a plan

You can import a plan you already have, in onboarding or from Plans → New plan → Import plan, by pasting text (for example a ChatGPT answer or a note) or choosing screenshots of another workout app. Only when you tap Paste or choose Screenshots on that screen, Trim sends what you pasted or chose to be read:

Before your first import is sent, Trim asks for your permission. If you choose Read on iPhone instead, that import is read on your iPhone and nothing is sent; Trim asks again next time. Once you allow it, Trim remembers your choice.

  • the pasted text and/or the screenshots you picked (downscaled JPEG images), over an encrypted HTTPS connection
  • to Trim's own server function, hosted by Vercel in the EU (Frankfurt), which passes it to Anthropic's Claude API to read the plan
  • and gets back only the plan's structure: day names, exercise names, sets and reps

No account, name, device ID or other identifier is sent with it, and nothing else from your iPhone: not your history, other plans, body check-ins or photo library. Trim only sees the screenshots you pick. Trim's server does not store or log the text or images. Like any web server, Vercel sees your IP address, which is used to limit how often imports can be requested and appears in its standard request logs.

Anthropic processes the content for us under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models. Anthropic automatically deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, or keeps them for up to 2 years if its automated safety systems flag them as violating its Usage Policy (Anthropic's retention policy).

If you're offline or the server can't be reached, Trim reads the plan on your iPhone with Apple's text recognition and sends nothing. The imported plan is then saved only on your iPhone, like any other plan. Nothing is sent unless you start an import.

Trim Pro purchases

Trim Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription. Apple processes the payment and handles your payment details; we never see them. Trim uses RevenueCat to process purchases and check whether Trim Pro is active. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random app user ID that Trim creates on your iPhone (not your name, email or Apple Account)
  • your Trim Pro purchase history: product, purchase and renewal dates, trial and expiry status, and App Store transaction details
  • when the Trim Pro screen was shown, and which one
  • the technical details any app request carries, such as IP address, device model, iOS and app version, and App Store country

RevenueCat never receives your plans, workouts, check-ins or other training data.

Product analytics

To learn which parts of Trim get used and where it can be simpler, Trim sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). PostHog receives:

  • a random ID that Trim creates on your iPhone (not your name, email or Apple Account)
  • which screens you open, and counts such as "a workout was finished with 4 exercises and 16 sets" or "a set was logged"
  • when the Trim Pro screen was shown and whether a purchase or restore succeeded
  • when a plan was imported: whether it came from text or screenshots, and how many days and exercises were read
  • device model, iOS and app version, and language

PostHog never receives exercise names, weights, reps, body measurements or any other training data. Trim does not record your screen, and PostHog is set to discard your IP address and not look up your location.

Apple

Apple distributes Trim, processes payments, and manages your subscription. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. See Apple's privacy policy.

What Trim does not use

  • No account or cloud sign-in
  • No advertising or tracking. Trim does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No HealthKit, location, contacts or camera, and no access to your photo library: Trim only receives the screenshots you pick for an import
  • No exercise databases, image servers or other third-party content services

Deleting your data

  • Completed workouts: Trim → Settings → Clear history, or delete one workout from History.
  • Plans: open the menu, then Plans → open a plan → … → Delete plan.
  • Everything Trim stores: delete the app from your iPhone.
  • Purchase records held by RevenueCat: email marvinbeckm@gmail.com and we'll delete them. Apple keeps its own record of your purchases.

Deleting the app does not cancel Trim Pro. Cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Workout data is processed on your device to provide the app (performance of a contract). Text and screenshots you import are processed through Vercel and Anthropic to read your plan, at your request (performance of a contract). Purchase data is processed through Apple and RevenueCat to sell and unlock Trim Pro (performance of a contract), and paywall views to understand whether the Trim Pro screen works (legitimate interest). Anonymous usage events are processed through PostHog to improve the app (legitimate interest). RevenueCat, Inc. (United States), PostHog Inc. (data stored in the EU), Vercel Inc. (United States; Trim's server runs in Frankfurt) and Anthropic, PBC (United States) process this data on our behalf.

Children

Trim is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your workout data in the app. For purchase data, you can ask for access, correction or deletion by email. You can also complain to your data protection authority. Email marvinbeckm@gmail.com for any privacy question.
