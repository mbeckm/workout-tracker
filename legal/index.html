Subscriptions

Trim Pro is an auto-renewing Apple subscription (monthly or yearly). Cancel or change it on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Restoring a purchase is in Trim → Settings → Restore Purchases.

Data

Workouts stay on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes that data. See the privacy policy.

Not medical advice

Trim is a logger. It does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe training.
