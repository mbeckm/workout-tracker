ScratchWorkout is provided by `[DEVELOPER_NAME]` (“ScratchWorkout”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains how the iOS app handles information.

## Workout data

Workout plans, exercise selections, logged sets, and workout history are stored locally on your iPhone. ScratchWorkout does not require or create a separate application account.

## Optional iCloud Sync

When iCloud is available, ScratchWorkout can store an encrypted workout snapshot in your private iCloud/CloudKit database. This is used only to back up your data and synchronize it between Apple devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

Private CloudKit data belongs to the iCloud user and is not visible to us through the CloudKit developer console. Apple processes iCloud data under its own terms and privacy policy. The data counts toward the user’s iCloud storage quota.

You can select **Delete iCloud Data** in ScratchWorkout’s iCloud Sync screen to remove the remote workout snapshot. This does not delete workouts stored locally on the current iPhone. You can remove local data by deleting the app and its data from the device.

## Exercise catalog

App Store builds use a built-in exercise catalog. Exercise search terms are not sent to a third-party exercise service. Development builds may use a separately configured catalog service, but those builds are not distributed through the App Store.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

ScratchWorkout does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Payments

If in-app purchases or subscriptions are introduced, payment processing will be handled by Apple through the App Store. ScratchWorkout will not receive full payment-card details. This policy and the App Store privacy disclosures must be updated before payment-related data handling changes.

## Data sharing and retention

We do not sell workout data. The current app has no developer-operated backend and does not share workout data with advertisers or data brokers.

Local data remains until you remove it or delete the app. The private iCloud snapshot remains until it is deleted from ScratchWorkout, removed through the user’s iCloud controls, or handled according to Apple’s iCloud retention practices.

## Children

ScratchWorkout is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not create user profiles or request a date of birth.

## Your choices

- Use ScratchWorkout locally without iCloud Sync.
- Enable or disable iCloud at the Apple Account/device level.
- Delete ScratchWorkout’s private iCloud snapshot in the app.
- Delete local ScratchWorkout data by deleting the app and its data.

## Changes

We may update this policy when ScratchWorkout’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will identify the current version.
